# Demo script

## The 90-second pitch

1. **Who:** the named user and their job.
2. **The pain today:** what they do now, and what it costs them.
3. **The trigger:** the moment they reach for this app.
4. **The aha:** the one answer that only this app can give.
5. **Why at scale:** what happens when every team has it.
