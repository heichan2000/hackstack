# PTO carryover is changing from November 1

*Posted in #announcements by Maria Santos, Head of People, on 29 October 2026.*

Hi all. After the engagement survey in September, the leadership team agreed to make PTO
carryover more generous. **From 1 November 2026:**

- You can carry over **up to 10 unused PTO days** into the next calendar year (it was 5).
- Carried-over days now **expire on June 30** of the new year (it was March 31).
- Nothing else changes: the allowance is still 25 days a year, and unused days beyond the limit
  are still not paid out unless local law requires it.

This applies to the days you have left at the end of 2026, so if you were rushing to use them
before December, you don't have to.

The handbook's PTO page still shows the old numbers. We'll update it in the next few weeks.
Until then, this announcement is the policy.

Questions go to me or #people-questions.

— Maria
