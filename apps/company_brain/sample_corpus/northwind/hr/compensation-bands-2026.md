# Compensation bands 2026

**Confidential: People Ops and Leadership only.** Owner: Maria Santos.

Base salary bands in USD for the Toronto benchmark. Other locations apply the location factor
in HiBob (Lisbon 0.72, Remote US 1.05).

| Level | Title | Band minimum | Band midpoint | Band maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E2 | Software Engineer | $98,000 | $112,000 | $126,000 |
| E3 | Senior Software Engineer | $128,000 | $146,000 | $164,000 |
| E4 | Staff Engineer | $165,000 | $186,000 | $207,000 |
| M1 | Engineering Manager | $160,000 | $180,000 | $200,000 |
| P2 | Product Manager | $118,000 | $134,000 | $150,000 |

## Rules

- New hires start between the band minimum and the midpoint.
- Anyone above the band maximum gets no base increase in the next cycle, only a one-off bonus.
- Bands were refreshed on 15 October 2026 from the Pave benchmark, up 4% on average.
