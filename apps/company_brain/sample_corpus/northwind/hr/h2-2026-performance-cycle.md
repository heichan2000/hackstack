# H2 2026 performance cycle

**Confidential: People Ops and Leadership only.** Owner: Maria Santos.

## Dates (updated 3 November 2026)

- **9 November:** self-reviews open in Lattice.
- **20 November:** self-reviews and peer feedback close.
- **24–26 November:** calibration sessions, by department. *(Moved from 17–19 November so they
  don't clash with the Lisbon offsite planning.)*
- **4 December:** managers share outcomes with their reports.
- **1 January 2027:** salary changes take effect.

## Calibration guidance

- Aim for about 15% "exceeds", 70% "meets" and at most 15% "below" per department, but don't force it.
- Promotions need a written case from the manager and one from a peer outside the team.
- The merit budget is 3.5% of base payroll. Promotions are funded separately.
