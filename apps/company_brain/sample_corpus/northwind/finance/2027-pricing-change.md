# 2027 pricing change

**Confidential until announced: Finance and Leadership only.** Owners: Dan Whitaker (Finance)
and Lena Fischer (Product).

Decided by the leadership team on **3 November 2026**. Customer announcement planned for
1 December 2026.

## What changes on 1 January 2027

- **Starter** goes from $49 to **$59 a month**.
- **Growth** goes from $299 to **$349 a month**, and now includes usage alerts for unlimited accounts.
- **Enterprise** list prices don't change; renewals after 1 January use the new rate card.
- Existing Starter and Growth customers keep their current price until their **first renewal
  after 1 April 2027**.

## Why

Infrastructure costs per account rose 14% this year, mostly from usage alerts and longer
retention on Growth. The change brings gross margin back above 75% by Q2 2027.

## Not yet decided

- Whether annual plans get a bigger discount (currently 15%).
- How sales handles deals already in flight. Dan and the head of sales will propose a rule by
  17 November.

Don't share this outside Finance and Leadership before the announcement.
