# Expenses and company card

Owner: Dan Whitaker (Head of Finance). Questions in #finance-help.

## What you can spend without asking

- Software and tools for your work, up to **$100 a month** per tool.
- Books and courses, up to **$1,000 a year** from your learning budget.
- A one-off home-office setup of **$1,500** in your first year.
- Meals while travelling for work, up to **$75 a day**.

Anything above these limits needs your manager's OK in writing (a Slack message is fine) before
you spend it.

## Receipts

Upload the receipt to Ramp **within 7 days** and add a one-line memo saying what it was for.
Cards with receipts missing for more than 30 days are frozen until they're sorted.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through Navan. Economy class for flights under 6 hours.

## What changed on 27 October 2026

The software limit went from $50 to $100 a month per tool, and Ramp now freezes cards after
30 days of missing receipts (it was 60).
