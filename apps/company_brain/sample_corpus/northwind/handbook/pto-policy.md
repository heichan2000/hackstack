# Paid time off (PTO) policy

Northwind Labs gives every full-time employee **25 days of paid time off per calendar year**,
on top of the public holidays of the country you work from. Part-time employees get a pro-rata
allowance. Your allowance resets on January 1.

## Booking time off

Book time off in HiBob at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days, and tell
your team in its Slack channel. You don't need your manager's approval for one or two days, but
check that your team still has cover for on-call and customer support.

## Carryover

You can carry over **up to 5 unused days** into the next calendar year. Carried-over days
**expire on March 31** of the new year: if you have not used them by then, they are gone. Days
beyond the 5-day limit are lost on January 1, and Northwind does not pay out unused PTO, except
where local law requires it.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO and is not capped. Tell your manager the same day. For absences
longer than five working days we may ask for a doctor's note.

## Parental leave

See the parental leave page in HiBob. It is separate from PTO and does not reduce your
allowance.
